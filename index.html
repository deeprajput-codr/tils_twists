🎮 How to Play

Tap a tile adjacent to the empty space to slide it.
On mobile, swipe the board to move tiles in that direction.
Use arrow keys on desktop.
Get all tiles in order (1, 2, 3… left-to-right, top-to-bottom) to win!
Earn up to ⭐⭐⭐ stars based on moves used.
